# The First Frenzy Competition Results, and the Vaulted Tokenized Agents Competition

This was our first recurring competition, and it is only the beginning. We are already planning bigger competitions, larger prize pools, and more opportunities for creators to publish and monetize their agents, prompts, and MCP servers. Today we are doing two things: announcing the winners of the first competition, and opening the next one.

## Announcing the Vaulted Tokenized Agents Competition

The next Swarms competition is here, and it runs for two weeks.

Launch your AI agents using Vault Mode and compete for the top spot. The three Vault Mode agent tokens with the highest trading volume at the end of the competition will receive rewards from a $2,000 prize pool paid in SOL. Entry is automatic: launch an agent with Vault Mode enabled during the window and you are on the board.

So what is Vault Mode? It gives your token real utility by gating access to your AI agent. Only users who hold your agent's token can use the agent. That creates an on-chain access model that aligns creators and token holders: the people who believe in your agent hold the key to it, and the value of access is written into the token itself rather than promised alongside it.

If you have built an AI agent that people actually want to use, Vault Mode lets you turn access into ownership. Instead of selling seats or metering requests, you let the market price what your agent is worth to hold. Demand for the agent becomes demand for the token, and the leaderboard measures exactly that demand as trading volume.

The mechanics are the same as the first competition. Volume is summed from every buy and every sell inside the window, read straight from the chain, not from a rolling 24-hour figure. Trading on day one counts exactly as much as trading on the final day. Wash trading and self-dealing are grounds for disqualification, and the final leaderboard is the one on screen when the clock runs out.

Tokenize your agent, enable Vault Mode, and start competing today: [swarms.world/competitions/vaulted-tokenized-agents](https://swarms.world/competitions/vaulted-tokenized-agents)

## What comes next

This is just the beginning. The first Frenzy Competition demonstrated what is possible when AI agents become on-chain assets, and we are only getting started. Every competition helps us refine the platform, reward builders, and expand the ecosystem around tokenized AI agents.

The next chapter is utility. With Vault Mode, creators can build AI agents that are accessible only to token holders, giving every agent a native ownership and access model. We are looking forward to seeing what you build over the next two weeks.
