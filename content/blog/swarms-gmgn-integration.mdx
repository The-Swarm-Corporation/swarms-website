# Swarms x GMGN: Discover and Trade Swarms Agents on GMGN

Swarms, the largest AI launchpad on Solana, is now integrated with [GMGN](https://gmgn.ai/?chain=sol).

We're excited to partner with GMGN to expand the visibility and discoverability of agents built on the Swarms Launchpad. You can now easily filter and discover Swarms agents directly within the GMGN trading platform, track SWRM ecosystem assets, and access the growing agent marketplace through a streamlined experience.

This integration connects two sides of the same market. Swarms is where AI agents are built, published, and tokenized. GMGN is where traders discover and act on new opportunities the moment they appear on-chain. Bringing them together means every agent launched on Swarms is now visible to one of the most active trading communities on Solana — with no extra work from the creator.

## What Is the Swarms Launchpad?

For readers coming from the GMGN side: the [Swarms Launchpad](https://swarms.world) is the discovery, distribution, and monetization layer built on top of the open-source `swarms` framework. Creators publish AI agents, prompts, and tools to the marketplace, and every listing can be launched one of three ways:

- **Free** — open for anyone to use.
- **Paid** — a fixed one-time price, payable by card or crypto.
- **Tokenized** — the listing mints a Solana token on a bonding curve. As demand grows, the token graduates to open DEX tradability, and the agent becomes a market of its own.

Tokenized agents are where this integration matters. Every tokenized agent on Swarms has a real contract address on Solana, live pricing, and creator fees that flow back to the builder on every trade. Holders aren't just speculating on a ticker — creators can enable **Vault Mode**, which gates the agent's actual capabilities to token holders only, turning the token into an access key for the underlying AI.

That combination — a working AI product with an on-chain market attached — is what GMGN users can now discover natively.

## What Is GMGN?

[GMGN](https://gmgn.ai/?chain=sol) is one of the most widely used trading platforms on Solana, built for discovering tokens early and acting on them fast. Its Trenches view surfaces new launches in real time, its analytics track smart-money flows and holder behavior, and its execution rails are built for speed.

For the Swarms ecosystem, that means tokenized agents get in front of traders at the exact moment they're most interesting: at launch, during bonding-curve progression, and at graduation.

## How Do You Find Swarms Agents on GMGN?

There are three ways to discover, track, and trade tokenized Swarms agents through GMGN:

### 1. Filter SWRM Agents From the Trenches Tab

Head to [GMGN's Trenches tab](https://gmgn.ai/?chain=sol) and filter for SWRM agents to browse everything launching across the Swarms ecosystem in real time.

The Trenches view is organized around the launch lifecycle — new pairs, tokens completing their bonding curves, and freshly graduated markets — so you can watch a Swarms agent move through every stage of its life as a token, from first mint to open DEX trading.

### 2. Search Any Agent by Contract Address

Looking for a specific agent? Paste its contract address into GMGN's search bar to instantly find it:

[https://gmgn.ai/sol/token/75Kzk967VVEKWXnYrehBWLFHT5yzmmz1izQv6q5Aswrm](https://gmgn.ai/sol/token/75Kzk967VVEKWXnYrehBWLFHT5yzmmz1izQv6q5Aswrm)

Every tokenized agent on the Swarms Launchpad displays its contract address directly on its listing page — copy it from the listing, drop it into GMGN, and you're looking at the agent's full trading view: price, liquidity, holders, and trade history.

### 3. Buy Through GMGN in One Click From the Launchpad

The integration works in both directions. On the Swarms Launchpad, every tokenized agent now has a **Buy on GMGN** button on its listing page — one click takes you from reading about an agent's capabilities to its GMGN trading view:

[https://swarms.world/prompt/bf5e1843-707b-4b06-a9e0-c030aa57d5ba](https://swarms.world/prompt/bf5e1843-707b-4b06-a9e0-c030aa57d5ba)

This closes the loop between research and execution. You can evaluate an agent where it lives — its description, use cases, reviews, and creator profile on swarms.world — and then trade it where the liquidity is, without hunting down the contract address yourself.

## Expanding Liquidity Across the Swarms Ecosystem

The integration with GMGN will help expand liquidity across the broader Swarms ecosystem.

By making Swarms tokenized agents easier to discover, track, and trade through GMGN, we're creating a more accessible market for agents across the ecosystem. Deeper discoverability compounds: more eyes on new launches means healthier bonding-curve progressions, faster graduations, tighter markets after graduation, and more creator fees flowing back to the builders who ship the agents.

That last part matters. On Swarms, trading activity isn't extractive — creators of tokenized agents earn fees from the markets their agents generate. Every new surface where those agents can be discovered and traded increases the incentive to build and launch serious agents on the platform.

This is another step toward building deeper infrastructure around the agent economy and increasing access to Swarms agents.

## Tracking the Ecosystem: The Swarms Screener

Alongside GMGN's views, the [Swarms Screener](https://swarms.world/screener) remains the native home for tracking the tokenized agent ecosystem — live launches, market caps, and volume across every agent token on the platform, with direct links to each agent's listing.

Screener and GMGN complement each other: the screener gives you the Swarms-native view with full agent context, while GMGN gives you the trader-native view with deep market analytics. Between the two, you can go from "what launched today" to "what does this agent actually do" to "execute the trade" in seconds.
