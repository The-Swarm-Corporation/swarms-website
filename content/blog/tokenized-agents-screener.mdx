# Introducing the Screener: Every Tokenized Agent on the Swarms Marketplace, Live on One Page

The agent economy now has a trading floor. We've launched the [Screener](https://swarms.world/screener) on the [Swarms Marketplace](https://swarms.world), a single page that shows every tokenized agent and prompt in the ecosystem with live on-chain data: normal launches, Frenzy Mode launches, and Vault Mode launches, all in one place.

If you want to know what the market is doing right now, this is the page you leave open.

## Why We Built It

Tokenized products have become the fastest-growing part of the marketplace. Creators launch agent tokens through Frenzy Mode to earn 1% of every trade, gate premium products behind token ownership with Vault Mode, and compete for real prize pools in our trading-volume competitions.

Until now, that activity was spread across the home page, the Frenzy hub, and individual competition leaderboards. The Screener pulls the entire tokenized field into one view, so traders, holders, and creators can see the whole market at a glance.

## Trending: The Top of the Market

The top of the page is a horizontal trending strip showing the highest market cap agent tokens in the ecosystem, ranked live. Each card carries the same information as our competition leaderboards:

- **Market cap** as the headline number, with the 24-hour price change beside it
- **24-hour trading volume**, read straight from on-chain data
- **The token's contract address**, one click to copy
- **The creator** behind the product and how long the token has been live
- **Mode badges** that tell you instantly whether a launch is Frenzy, Vault, or both

Scroll right to walk down the rankings, exactly like the Frenzy strip on the marketplace home page.

## Latest Launches: The Live Feed

Below trending is the part we're most excited about. The Latest Launches section lists every tokenized product newest first, and it updates in near real-time.

When someone launches a new tokenized agent anywhere on the marketplace, the card pops onto the page within seconds for everyone watching. It animates in at the top of the feed with a pulsing NEW badge and a red glow, already quoted with its bonding-curve market cap. No refresh needed.

That means the Screener doubles as a launch radar. If you want to be early on new agent tokens, this is where they surface first.

## Filter by How the Token Was Launched

A simple filter bar lets you slice the field by launch mechanics:

- **All tokens**: the full tokenized catalog
- **Frenzy Mode**: launches where the creator earns 1% of every buy and sell
- **Vault Mode**: products gated behind token ownership, where usage demand becomes buy pressure
- **Frenzy + Vault**: launches running both at once, the most aggressive creator configuration on the platform

Each filter shows a live count, so you can see how the ecosystem splits across launch modes at any moment.

## Where the Data Comes From

Every number on the Screener is real market data, not marketplace vanity metrics. Market caps, prices, 24-hour volume, and price changes are read from on-chain sources through Jupiter, the same data pipeline that powers our [competition leaderboards](https://swarms.world/competitions). If a token trades, the Screener knows.

## For Creators: Your Launch Now Has an Audience

If you publish tokenized products, the Screener changes what a launch feels like. The moment your agent token goes live, it appears at the top of Latest Launches for every person on the page, with your name on the card. Climb the market cap rankings and you graduate into the trending strip.

Ready to be on the board?

1. [Publish an agent or prompt](https://swarms.world/launch) on the marketplace
2. Launch it as a token, and choose Frenzy Mode, Vault Mode, or both
3. Watch it appear on the [Screener](https://swarms.world/screener) in seconds

## Try It Now

The Screener is live today at [swarms.world/screener](https://swarms.world/screener), linked directly from the marketplace sidebar. Open it, leave it running, and watch the agent economy trade in real time.
